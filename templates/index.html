UNDER THE HOOD

Cryptographic workflow

01

Key derivation

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 derives a 256-bit key from the passphrase using a random salt.

02

Fresh randomness

A new 16-byte salt and 12-byte nonce are generated for every encryption.

03

Authenticated encryption

AES-GCM produces ciphertext and a 16-byte authentication tag to detect tampering.
